## 🪝 The Hook

## 📜 Complete Narrative Script
- **Scene 1** `(6.0s)`: For over a thousand years, historians thought this city was merely a myth invented by the Greeks.
- **Scene 2** `(6.5s)`: Before Alexandria existed, Heracleion was Egypt's undisputed gateway to the Mediterranean and the trade capital of the ancient world.
- **Scene 3** `(6.5s)`: Until the eighth century, when catastrophic soil liquefaction and tidal waves dragged the entire metropolis beneath the sea in hours.
- **Scene 4** `(7.0s)`: Swallowed thirty feet deep and lost under thick mud, it vanished from human memory for twelve straight centuries.
- **Scene 5** `(6.0s)`: Until the year two thousand, when marine explorers located sixty-four ships, gold treasures, and towering five-meter god statues.
- **Scene 6** `(6.0s)`: Atlantis might be legend, but the sunken city of Heracleion was terrifyingly real.
